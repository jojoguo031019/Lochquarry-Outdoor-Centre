Land Based Activities

Visitors can take part in a wide range of land-based activities on and off site. Whether your group wants a gentle walk around the grounds, a challenging day in the hills or a fun competition on the archery range, there is something for everyone. All of our activities are led by our experienced instructors and can be adapted to suit the age and experience of your group. Read on to find out more about each activity, including age limits and group sizes.

Hillwalking

From short walks around the site to Munro-bagging expeditions, Lochquarry has it all! Walks can be tailored to suit any age or experience of groups and can last from one hour to all day adventures.

  • Max group size 12
  • Ages 6+

Archery

Are you the next Robin Hood? Learn to hold a bow and fire an arrow and take part in fast and fun shootout competitions.

  • Max group size 6
  • Ages 6+

Orienteering

Set in the centre's grounds, find all the markers and make it back in time to show off your superior navigation knowledge.

  • Groups of 2+, one map per group
  • Ages 6+

Axe Throwing

Take yourself back to a time of Vikings and have a go at throwing an axe. Try to hit the target, better yet throw yourself a bullseye.

  • Max group size 6
  • Ages 10+
